# Executive Intelligence Reporting Engine

## 1. Objective
Produce concise, authoritative, and non-technical intelligence reports tailored for law enforcement investigators, bank fraud analysts, and executive decision-makers.

## 2. Language Rules & Jargon Reduction
- Avoid raw machine learning terminology in summaries (e.g. avoid *"Random Forest predicted class 4"*).
- Use clear operational language (e.g. *"The system identified Zone 04 as the highest-probability cash-out area with 81.4% confidence."*).
- Clearly demarcate **Prediction**, **Candidate Ranking**, and **Actual Evidence**.

## 3. Mandatory Disclaimers
Every brief automatically includes the Academic Synthetic Research disclosure:
> *"All complaint records and historical activity are synthetic. Predicted zones and candidate rankings represent mathematical likelihoods. Candidate ATMs are NOT confirmed locations of withdrawal; physical confirmation requires banking interchange logs and surveillance verification."*

## 4. Report Contents
1. **Case & Incident Overview**: Fraud type, financial amount, victim bank, and reported locality.
2. **Geographic Cash-Out Prediction**: Top zone and statistical model confidence.
3. **Candidate ATM Points**: Table of candidate ATMs with distance, bank operator, and candidate score.
4. **Key Contributing Signals**: Plain-language breakdown of factors influencing the recommendation.
5. **Recommended Law Enforcement Focus**: Practical next steps (CCTV retrieval, transaction reconciliation, cell tower queries).
6. **Official Reporting Reference**: Links to cybercrime.gov.in and Helpline 1930.
